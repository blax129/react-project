# Lagos world research and implementation

Research date: 7 October 2026.

This is an original, stylized canvas interpretation of a Lagos journey, not a photographic reconstruction or navigation map. Historical photographs inform recognizable structures; they do not establish present-day street conditions. The tour takes detours and uses equal 2,200-unit stages. Airport Road remains the endless final area after stage 30.

## Evidence and artistic interpretation

Strong landmark cues include Festac’s entrance gateway, NIIT’s documented first-floor location at Damilola Plaza on 22 Road, the LASU entrance, Iyana Iba’s elevated interchange, Computer Village electronics frontage, Pen Cinema transport infrastructure, and Alausa park greenery. [FHA restoration reporting](https://fha.gov.ng/news/restoration-festac-management) additionally supports First Avenue solar lighting. [Festac commercial reporting](https://thesun.ng/festac-from-residential-area-to-commercial-hub/) supports the housing, shops and car displays on the estate roads.

The district maps and road catalogue establish locality and corridor context, not exact building facades. Amuwo Odofin, Abule Ado, Egbeda, Akowonjo and Dopemu use interpretive neighbourhood architecture. Satellite storage tanks, generic civic halls, hotel names, school walls, shop signs, trees and parked vehicles are artistic motifs, not surveyed individual properties. Trade Fair pavilion shapes simplify visual references from [the trade fair website](https://www.nitf.ng/). The airport is an original terminal/tower silhouette, not an accurate airfield plan.

All hills, potholes, mud, debris, fuel leaks and police checkpoint placements are fictional gameplay obstacles. Their assignment to a location makes no claim about actual road quality, police activity or neighbourhood safety. No external photographs were copied into game assets.

## Implementation

- `src/game/areas.js`: thirty stable IDs, names, signs, colour palettes and scene/road/hazard profiles.
- `src/game/scenery.js`: deterministic parallax scenes; at most four 960×320 cached canvases, approximately 4.9 MB of pixel buffers. No added background image downloads.
- `src/game/terrain.js`: locally varied road shapes with smooth stage boundaries; existing distance-based escalation retained.
- `src/game/hazards.js`: regional hazards complement the global schedule, avoiding nearby duplicates; debris routes include repair opportunities. The opening five stages retain the established learning progression.
- `src/components/RouteGuide.jsx`: accessible preview of all thirty scenes, without teleporting or changing leaderboard distance.

Distinct areas reuse a shared architectural vocabulary and obstacle mechanics. They do not each introduce a separate physics mechanic. Automated checks cover route boundaries and existing driving behaviour; they are not proof that every vehicle can finish all thirty stages.
